Fiido passenger-facing hand warmer

Experimental soft hood, open toward the passenger. The palm approaches the rail from above and inside; the roof must lift freely. All defaults are estimates. Make a scrap mockup before cutting good fabric.

1. Orientation

Cross-section looking along the rail; schematic, not to scale. The full inward edge stays open. No cuff. Two hook-and-loop tabs attach at the ends, outside the gripping zone.

2. Measurements (mm)

3. Cutting pattern

Solid = cut; long dashes = stitch; short dashes = fold. Shell and lining use the same starting template; trim lining after scrap fitting if needed.

4. Assembly

  1. Print at actual size, check the 50 mm bar, trim page borders and butt pages in row/column order.
  2. Cut one scrap wrap and two end panels. Mark roof/outer wall/floor folds. Sew the three straight cap edges to each wrap end; leave the entire inward mouth open.
  3. Check the notches fit over the rail from inside. With the passenger seated on a stationary bike, check natural grip and immediate withdrawal upward and inward. Adjust the opening before continuing.
  4. Repeat in weather-resistant shell and wool fleece. Nest lining and shell, adjust bulk, bind mouth and notches. Leave a small drainage opening at the lowest point.
  5. Box-stitch two webbing tabs to reinforced outer end panels near the notches; wrap around the tube and sew hook-and-loop at the measured overlap. Keep hooks away from skin and all tails captive.
  6. Check rotation, sliding, entry/exit and wheel clearance. Do not ride with an unvalidated prototype. No wrist restraints.
