Passenger hand warmer for the Fiido T2 Family Add-on

A pair of insulated covers that sit over the upper side rails, so a passenger can hold the rail with warm hands. Enter a few measurements, print the pattern on A4 at full size, then sew.

Prototype. This is not a certified passenger safety accessory. No straps go around the hand or wrist, and the cover is open underneath. Before any ride, check with the bike standing still that the passenger can grip naturally and pull the hand out in any direction, and that nothing can reach the wheel.

3. Measurements

Measure with a ruler, tape or calipers. For the hand, have the passenger wear their winter gloves and grip a broom handle or dowel about as thick as the rail.

4. Pattern pieces

Solid line: cut. Dashed line: stitch. Small notches: match marks. Dashed grey lines show the A4 page joins.

5. Print and export

Print on A4 at 100 % / “Actual size”. Turn off “Fit to page”. Page 1 has the 50 × 50 mm check square, a page map and trimming instructions.

6. Materials and sewing

Sewing instructions (make two)

  1. Print the pattern and check the 50 mm square. Cut out the paper pieces on the solid lines.
  2. Cut, for the pair: 2 shell wraps (P01), 4 shell end caps (P02), 2 lining wraps (P04), 4 lining end caps (P05) and 4 webbing tabs (P03). Transfer all match marks with a 3 mm snip into the seam allowance. Seal webbing ends with a lighter.
  3. Test first. Sew one shell from scrap fabric (steps 4–5), slip it over the rail with the bike standing still, and check the fit with the passenger’s gloved hand before cutting good fabric.
  4. Shell. With right sides together, pin one end cap to one stitch-line edge of the shell wrap. Match the centre marks (and the skirt marks, if any). Ease the wrap around the curve and sew on the stitch line. Repeat at the other end.
  5. Clip the curved seam allowances every 10 mm, without cutting the stitches. Turn right side out.
  6. Lining. Make the lining the same way from P04 and P05. Do not turn it: its seam allowances stay outside, facing the shell. The lining is cut slightly smaller so it sits inside without wrinkles.
  7. Slide the lining into the shell, wrong sides together. Line up the open lower edges and the notches, and baste them 3 mm from the edge.
  8. Bind the whole open edge in one pass: along both long sides, across each end-cap base and around each rail notch. Fold the ends of the binding under where they meet.
  9. Tabs. Sew hook tape to one face of the “hook + loop” end of each tab. Box-stitch the “sew to cap” end to the outside of an end cap, right beside the rail notch, through both layers (one tab per end cap). Wrap the tab around a dowel the size of the rail to find where its end lands, and sew loop tape there.
  10. Fit check, bike standing still: fit over the rail, close both tabs, and confirm all of the following. The passenger can grip naturally. The hand comes out immediately in every direction. The cover does not slide or rotate. Nothing hangs near the wheel. Trim any tab tails.

Record what worked and what did not, together with the real measurements, before changing the pattern.
